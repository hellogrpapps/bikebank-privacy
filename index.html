2. What BikeBank does (plain language)

BikeBank helps you track bike-related activity and estimate money you may save by cycling instead of other transport (or similar savings framing in the app).

For App Store review and early use, BikeBank is designed to work in mock mode by default:

  • You can explore the app with demo / local sample activity and savings data.
  • You are not required to create a cloud account or connect Strava to use the mock demo.
  • Optional features (email/password account via Supabase; Strava OAuth) may exist in the product but are not required for the mock-mode experience described here.
