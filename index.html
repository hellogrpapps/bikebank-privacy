2. What BikeBank does

BikeBank helps you track bike-related activity and estimate money you may save by cycling instead of other transport.

You can use BikeBank in mock mode:

  • You can explore the app with demo and local sample activity and savings data.
  • You do not need a cloud account or a Strava connection to use mock mode.
  • Email and password sign-in (through Supabase) and Strava are optional. Both stay off unless you choose them later.

3.2 Account sign-in (optional)

Email and password sign-in through Supabase is optional and stays off unless you choose it later. If you create an account, BikeBank may collect:

3.3 Strava (optional)

Strava stays off unless you choose to connect it later. Mock mode does not use Strava, and the current app does not collect fitness or location data. If you later connect Strava, BikeBank may receive:

CategoryWhat may be collected
Fitness / activity dataActivity data you authorize BikeBank to receive from Strava, such as rides, distances, durations, and related fields Strava provides under the access you approve.
Location-related data from StravaStrava activities may include route or location-related information. BikeBank does not collect device GPS. If you connect Strava and an activity includes location-related fields, those fields come from Strava.
Account linkageIf you are signed in, your BikeBank account may be linked to the Strava tokens needed to keep the connection working.

You can use BikeBank without connecting Strava.

6. Storage and transfers outside Canada

  • Mock mode and other local data stay on your device unless you later choose to sync.
  • Supabase, if you choose an account, may process or store information on servers outside Canada, including in the United States.
  • Strava, if you choose to connect it, processes information under its own privacy policy and may do so outside Canada.

When optional cloud services process information outside Canada, we remain responsible for the personal information we handle under applicable Canadian privacy law.

8. Children’s privacy

BikeBank is not directed at children. It is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a higher age of digital consent applies, do not use optional account or Strava features unless a parent or guardian is involved as the law requires.

9. Your choices

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold, and you may withdraw consent for optional processing such as a Strava connection. Contact contact@grpapps.com.
